## Position

I build financial data products that connect domain research, AI agents, and usable interfaces.

My strongest theme is not "AI demos." It is turning repeated financial research work into structured systems:

- source-backed company research and valuation
- PB/WM market briefing automation
- Korean financial data and MCP infrastructure
- CUFA research operations and education assets
- career, real asset, and Slack operations dashboards
